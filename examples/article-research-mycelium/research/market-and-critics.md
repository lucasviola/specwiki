# Market landscape and critic angles

## Bull case (collect quotes)

- Bans and fees on single-use plastics raise the floor for alternatives
- Brands want an unboxing story that is not “more cardboard”
- Agricultural waste streams need higher-value sinks

## Bear case (do not skip)

- **Unit cost** still loses to EPS at volume for many SKUs
- **Lead time** clashes with fast fashion / electronics spikes
- **Performance** (moisture, drop tests) is application-specific — one viral demo ≠ all shipping
- “Compostable” claims confuse consumers when local facilities cannot process the material

## Questions for the cost section

1. At what order volume does mycelium beat EPS **including** disposal fees?
2. Who owns the mold tooling — brand or grower?
3. What happens to scrap that fails QC — compost, animal bedding, landfill?

## Skeptic line to pressure-test

> If the pack needs a plastic mailer bag to stay dry, have we only moved the problem?
