# Angle and thesis

## Working thesis

Mycelium packaging is less a miracle material than a **logistics bet**: it wins when shipping weight, compostability rules, and brand storytelling line up — and stalls when factories need speed polystyrene already mastered.

## Reader promise

By the end of the piece, a curious non-scientist should understand:

1. What mycelium foam actually is (and is not)
2. Why growing a mailer takes days, not minutes
3. Where the economics break today

## Tone

Curious, lightly skeptical, concrete. Prefer factory floor and compost bin over hype decks.

## Openings to try

- A warehouse that stopped shredding foam peanuts
- A designer unboxing a lamp nested in white fungal foam that looks like styrofoam’s quieter cousin
- A compost pile two months later: the pack is gone; the plastic tape is not
