# Biology primer (for writers)

## Mycelium vs mushroom

- **Mycelium** — the underground (or substrate-bound) network of fungal filaments
- **Mushroom** — the fruiting body; usually _not_ what packaging companies grow for foam

For packaging, growers feed mycelium agricultural waste (hemp hurd, corn stover, sawdust). The fungus binds the particles into a light, insulating block. Growth is then **stopped with heat** so it does not keep eating the box in transit.

## Facts to verify before publish

- Typical growth time in days for a protective corner block
- Whether home compost is enough, or industrial composting is required
- Fire / moisture performance vs EPS foam for the specific product class

## Words to avoid without sources

- “Plastic-eating”
- “Fully circular”
- “Infinite scale”
