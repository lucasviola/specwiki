# Interview questions

## For a materials scientist

1. What strain traits matter most for packaging strength vs growth speed?
2. How do you validate that heat treatment fully stops growth?
3. Which failure mode should journalists stop oversimplifying?

## For a packaging engineer at a brand

1. Which SKU was the first mycelium pilot, and why that one?
2. What test did the material fail before it passed?
3. Did unboxing metrics or sustainability OKRs drive the trial?

## For a municipal compost operator

1. Do you accept mycelium packaging today? Under what labeling?
2. What contamination do you actually see in residential bins?
3. What would make these packs easier to process at your facility?

## For a cost-conscious skeptic (logistics lead)

1. Where did the landed cost break the business case?
2. Would a plastic tax change your mind next year?
3. What alternative (paper pulp, molded fiber, reuse totes) are you betting on instead?
