# Manufacturing notes

## Simplified process

1. **Inoculate** sterile agricultural waste with selected fungal strains
2. **Pack** the mix into molds (mailer shapes, corner blocks, lamp cushions)
3. **Grow** in controlled humidity/temperature for several days
4. **Dry / heat-treat** to kill the organism and lock the shape
5. **Trim / finish** and ship to the brand’s pack-out line

## Why it feels slow

Polystyrene expands in seconds. Mycelium **occupies calendar time**. That is fine for premium goods with planned inventory; painful for same-day high-SKU fulfillment.

## Design constraints worth quoting

- Wall thickness vs insulation vs growth time
- Mold reuse and cleaning between batches
- Water use and contamination risk on the production floor

## Scene ideas

- Walking a grow room that smells faintly like a forest floor after rain
- Hearing HVAC before you see the racks of white blocks
