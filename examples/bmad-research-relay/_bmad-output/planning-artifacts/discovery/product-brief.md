# Relay Desk — Product Brief (mock)

## One-liner

Turn daily standup notes into a browsable local status board that agents and humans can both edit.

## Who it is for

Small teams already living in git and markdown who do not want another SaaS board.

## Success signal

A developer can run `relay add` from a repo, commit `.relay/`, and a teammate can list the same notes after pull — without an account.
