# Product — Parcel Path

Parcel Path is a pretend TypeScript CLI that answers: **where is my package, and
what should I do next?**

Shoppers paste a carrier tracking id. The CLI resolves a carrier adapter, prints
a short timeline, and ends with one plain-language next action — not a raw
status enum.

## Public demo

Offline fixture id: `DEMO-1001` (under `tests/fixtures/`).

## Out of scope for v0

- Multi-package batch tracking
- Push notifications
- Carrier account login
- Label printing or full shipping ops
